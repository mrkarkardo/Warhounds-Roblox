# WARHOUNDS art brief (for the imaging teammate)

WARHOUNDS is a fast 6v6 first-person shooter on Roblox. Two teams: **Hounds** vs **Jackals**.
Look: grounded modern military realism (muted, gritty, real places like ports, rail yards,
villages, power plants, markets) with blocky Roblox characters.

## Colours
| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background / dark | `#1A1B1F` |
| Main light (off-white) | `#EBE8E1` |
| **Accent, the only bright colour (use sparingly)** | `#FFB02E` |
| Team Hounds | `#4696FF` |
| Team Jackals | `#FF783C` |
| Secondary text | `#8C8C88` |

## Paste this at the end of every prompt
```
Style: grounded modern military realism, muted desaturated palette, charcoal #1A1B1F and
off-white #EBE8E1 with a single amber-orange #FFB02E accent used sparingly. Gritty, cinematic,
original design. Characters are blocky Roblox-style figures wearing realistic military gear.
No blood or gore, no real-world brand logos or military insignia, no text unless asked.
```

## Rules every image must pass
- **Roblox moderation:** no blood/gore, no real people, no real brand or gun-maker logos, no real
  military insignia or flags, nothing sexual, no hate symbols. Guns are fine.
- **Original:** never copy another game's logo, characters, maps or UI.
- **Check AI mistakes before sending:** misspelled text, extra fingers, melted guns, gibberish signs.
- **Characters must look like Roblox** (blocky head, rectangular limbs).
- **Textures must tile seamlessly**, flat even lighting, straight-on, no shadows or perspective.

## Sizes
Thumbnails 1920×1080 · icon 512×512 · map vote pictures 1280×720 · textures 1024×1024 ·
emblems 1024×1024 transparent PNG · wall posters 2000×900.
